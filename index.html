WHY SHARED?

The market does not reset when you leave.

Prices are generated from UTC time, so the same symbol at the same moment resolves to the same synthetic price for everyone. Reopen Tickrift later and the market has moved forward instead of restarting from your last browser session.

PLAYER FLOW

Tiny crowd effects, not instant pump buttons.

With the optional global backend enabled, filled paper buys and sells add a very small temporary pressure to the shared simulated price. One user barely matters; a large crowd moving in one direction can become visible.

Simulation only.

Tickrift is a game and practice tool. It does not accept deposits, hold assets, connect to a brokerage account or execute real trades. Virtual funds and simulated assets have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn.
