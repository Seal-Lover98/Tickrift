Tickrift is a virtual-money market simulation and educational game. It is not a broker, exchange, wallet, bank or investment adviser.

No real securities, funds or crypto assets are bought or sold. Virtual funds and virtual assets have no monetary value and cannot be deposited, transferred or withdrawn.

No guarantee of real-world results

Synthetic prices, fills, fees, liquidity and player-flow effects are simplified game mechanics. Results in Tickrift should not be treated as evidence of how a real investment or trade would perform.
