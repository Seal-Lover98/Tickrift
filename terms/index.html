TERMS

Terms for a simulation, not a brokerage.

These terms describe the intended use and limits of the Tickrift simulator.

Simulation only. Tickrift does not accept deposits, hold customer money, connect to a brokerage account or execute real trades. Virtual funds and simulated profits have no cash value.

What Tickrift is

Tickrift is an educational market simulator for practicing the mechanics of short-term paper trading and long-term paper investing. It is not a broker, exchange, bank, investment adviser or portfolio manager.

Market data and simulated execution

Prices can be labelled MARKET, DELAYED or SIMULATED. Provider-linked prices can still differ from prices available on another venue or at another moment. Simulated fills, spreads, fees, slippage, stops and liquidation rules are simplified and cannot reproduce every real-world execution condition.

Do not treat simulator performance as a promise of future results.

No personalized investment advice

Tickrift provides tools for a simulation. It does not tell a user which real security or crypto asset they should buy, sell or hold based on their personal situation. Educational explanations describe how the simulator works rather than making a recommendation about a real portfolio.

Acceptable use

Do not attempt to interfere with the service, overload its market-data endpoints, extract protected provider data in bulk, bypass technical restrictions or use the site in a way that violates applicable law or third-party data licences.

Availability

The simulator may change, be unavailable, lose locally stored data or fall back to simulated prices if a market-data provider is unavailable. Users should not rely on Tickrift for time-critical real-world financial decisions.
