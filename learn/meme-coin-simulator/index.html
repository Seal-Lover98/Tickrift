Meme coin practice without real tokens

Tickrift includes a dedicated meme coin simulator for high-volatility paper trading practice with virtual money. The category is separate from major crypto so the experience stays clear.

No real tokens, wallets or deposits. Meme coin prices in Tickrift are synthetic game values unless a future feed is explicitly labelled otherwise.

What is in the Meme tab?

The practice list includes widely followed meme-themed tickers such as DOGE, SHIB, PEPE, BONK, WIF, FLOKI, PENGU and BOME, plus other simulated instruments. Their volatility settings are intentionally higher than the stock and index categories to make risk controls more visible during practice.

Why keep meme coins separate?

Meme coins often behave differently from large stocks or diversified funds. A separate category makes it easier to compare higher-volatility paper trades without presenting those tokens as equivalent to broad-market investments.

Use the simulator, not hype, as the point

The goal is to practise order mechanics, position sizing and exits with virtual money. Tickrift does not tell users which token to buy and does not provide real-money execution.
