LONG-TERM INVESTING

Practice building a virtual portfolio.

Tickrift keeps long-term paper holdings separate from short-term simulated trades.

Invest mode is separate from Trade mode. It is designed for holding virtual assets over time rather than opening short-term leveraged paper positions.

What Invest mode tracks

Each virtual investment has a quantity and an average cost. Tickrift compares that average cost with the displayed current price to calculate the paper market value and unrealized return.

HoldingsAverage cost

Additional paper buys update the weighted average purchase cost of the holding.

PortfolioAllocation

The portfolio view shows how much simulated value is concentrated in each holding.

RoutineRecurring paper buys

A local recurring simulation can rehearse regular investing while the simulator is running.

MarketsStocks, ETFs and crypto

Use the market-data badge to see whether the selected instrument is provider-linked or simulated.

ETFs, funds and indices are not the same thing

An index is a benchmark calculation. An ETF or fund is an investable product that can be designed to track an index or another strategy. Tickrift lists indices for market context and lists ETFs/funds separately so users can see the distinction inside the simulator.

What the simulator does not prove

Past or simulated returns do not establish what an investment will do in the future. The simulator also does not account for every tax rule, fund cost, currency effect, dividend treatment or corporate action. It is a practice environment, not a personal investment plan.
