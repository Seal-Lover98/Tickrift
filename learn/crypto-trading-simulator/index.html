CRYPTO SIMULATOR

Practise crypto trading in a synthetic market

The Tickrift crypto trading simulator provides a synthetic 24/7-style market for virtual trading practice. You can explore major crypto tickers without buying, depositing or withdrawing any cryptocurrency.

Simulation only: the default prices are synthetic, not exchange quotes. Nothing shown on Tickrift is a real crypto balance.

Crypto paper trading

Practise virtual long and short positions, risk controls and multiple chart timeframes using the same order workflow as the stock simulator. Because the market is generated from time, it keeps moving after you close the page and catches up when you return.

Major crypto and meme coins are separated

Major digital assets have their own Crypto category, while higher-volatility meme-themed tokens are placed in a separate Meme tab. This keeps the instrument list easier to understand and avoids mixing every token into one long menu.

Shared player flow

If the global-flow backend is configured, paper buys and sells can create tiny temporary pressure in the synthetic price. The effect is deliberately small and fades over time; the feature is designed as game feedback, not as a representation of real exchange order books.
