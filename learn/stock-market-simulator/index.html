STOCK MARKET SIMULATOR

Practise stocks without risking real money

Tickrift's stock market simulator lets you practise paper trading and long-term virtual investing with familiar company tickers while every order remains simulated.

No shares are purchased. Company names and tickers identify simulated instruments only. Tickrift does not connect to a brokerage account.

Practise both trading and investing

Use the Trade order mode for short-term paper long or short positions. Use Invest for unleveraged virtual holdings with an average cost and portfolio value. Both modes draw from the same paper cash balance, making it easier to see how different decisions compete for capital inside the simulation.

Timeframes from one minute to one week

The chart supports 1-minute, 5-minute, 15-minute, 30-minute, 1-hour, 2-hour, 4-hour, daily and weekly candles. Each button changes the candle duration and the historical window instead of merely relabelling the same chart.

Stocks included

The simulator includes a broad practice list across technology, banks, payments, consumer companies, healthcare, energy, industrials and selected international ADRs. Search by company name or ticker inside the terminal, or build a local watchlist.
